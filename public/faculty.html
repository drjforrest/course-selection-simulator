How this page works

What your changes do

The student simulator uses this information to decide which courses students can place in each term. Changes are saved on the server and apply to every student who opens or reloads the simulator after you save. Plans already open in a student's browser are not updated until they reload.

Students never see this page, and nothing students do is stored here.

Where defaults come from

You don't have to fill in every term. For each course and term the simulator uses the first of these that exists:

  1. A one-term exception for that exact term (the One-term exceptions tab).
  2. The course's schedule set with Change schedule (the When courses run tab), from its starting term onward.
  3. The program model for the intake courses: HINF 5101 runs every Spring and Fall, and HINF 5105 and HINF 5106 every Spring, in all formats.
  4. The course schedule spreadsheet (Spring 2025 to Fall 2026) for the same season, most recent year first.

The Source column shows which one a row is using. Courses with no data at all are shown to students as Unconfirmed and can still be placed, with a warning.

Changing when a course runs

In When courses run, click Change schedule on the course's row. For Spring, Summer and Fall, tick the formats it runs in; leave a season blank if it doesn't run then. Choose Starting, the first term the change applies to, and save. The change repeats every year from then on. Use original schedule removes it.

One-term exceptions

  1. In One-term exceptions, choose the Term.
  2. Tick each format a course runs in that term, or clear every box to mark it not offered. Changed rows turn yellow.
  3. Click Save changes. Switching terms or leaving the page without saving asks before discarding edits.

Use default on a row removes that exception. Reset whole term to default removes every exception for the term.

Formats and program versions

Boston on-ground students can take on-ground, hybrid and online sections. Online and Vancouver students can take online sections only. A course that runs on-ground or hybrid only is unavailable to them that term.

To make a course available to online students, tick Online.

Credits & prerequisites

The second tab holds each course's credits and prerequisites, taken from the 2026–2027 catalogue. Edit them only when the catalogue changes. Revert restores the catalogue values.

Students cannot place a course until its prerequisites are completed or planned in an earlier term.

Signing out

You stay signed in for up to 8 hours on this browser. Use Sign out on shared computers. After 10 wrong passwords, sign-in from that address is blocked for 15 minutes.
