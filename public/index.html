MSHI Course Selection Simulator

MS in Health Informatics · no concentration · Boston, Online, Vancouver

Plan your MS in Health Informatics

Use this simulator to lay out the courses you will take in each term until you graduate. It checks your plan against the program rules as you go, then gives you a roadmap to print and discuss with your academic advisor.

  1. Tell us about you. Choose your program version and the term you start planning from.
  2. Mark courses you have already completed, if any.
  3. Build your plan. Place courses into terms by dragging them, or click a course to pick a term. The tracker shows what you still need.
  4. Print your roadmap and take it to your advisor.

What the degree requires

  • 33 to 36 credits in total. Most courses are 3 credits; some are 4.
  • Three core courses: HINF 5101 Introduction to Health Informatics; HINF 5105 The American Healthcare System (Boston and Online) or HINF 5106 The Canadian Healthcare System (Vancouver); and HINF 7701 Capstone.
  • Two courses from each of four pools: Business Management, Technical, Health Informatics, and General Electives.
  • A grade of B– or higher in every course and a cumulative GPA of at least 3.000.

Nothing is saved. Your plan exists only in this browser tab. Logging out, closing the tab or refreshing the page erases it, so print or save it as a PDF before you leave.

This is a planning aid, not registration. Future terms are projected from past schedules and faculty updates and can change. Your advisor has the final word on your plan of study.

2. Courses you have already taken

Tick every course you have already finished with a grade of B– or higher, and any transfer credit the program has approved (for example, BCIT DIGH 7000 transfers as HINF 5101). Ticked courses count toward your credits and requirements, and satisfy prerequisites. If you are a new student, leave everything blank.

Other prerequisite coursework lists courses from outside this program that satisfy a prerequisite here (for example, MATH 5010 for PHTH 6210). Tick them only if you have completed them; they do not count toward your degree credits.

You can come back and change any of this later with Edit setup.
How to use the planner

1. Read the tracker

The boxes at the top show your progress. Credits must end between 33 and 36. Core needs all three core courses. Each pool needs two courses. A box turns green when it is complete, and the wide box at the end tells you exactly what is still missing.

The simulator decides which requirement each course counts toward, choosing whatever fills the most requirements. You don't need to assign them yourself.

2. Add courses

Drag a course from the course list (beside or above the terms, depending on screen size) onto a term. Or click a course (or press Enter on it) to read its description and pick a term from a list; terms it can't go in are greyed out with the reason.

To see what fits a particular term, choose it under Show what fits in, or click that term's heading. Courses that can't go there are dimmed, with the reason on the card. Click the heading again, or choose Any term, to clear it.

Use the search box and the pool buttons to narrow the list.

3. Move or remove courses

Drag a course from one term to another. To remove it, click its ×, drag it back onto the course list, or select it and press Delete.

HINF 5101 and your healthcare-systems course are placed for you in the first term each runs. You can move them like any other course.

+ Add a term extends your plan if you need more time. Remove last empty term trims unused terms at the end.

4. Rules the planner enforces

The planner will not let you make these moves, and tells you why:

  • Going over 36 credits.
  • Taking a course before its prerequisite. The prerequisite must be completed or planned in an earlier term, not the same one.
  • Placing a course in a term where it isn't offered, or isn't offered in a format you can take. Boston students can take on-ground, hybrid and online sections. Online and Vancouver students can take online sections only.
  • Taking the capstone before completing three semesters.

5. Warnings (advice only)

Yellow notes in Plan check, below the terms, are advice. Your plan still works, but talk to your advisor:

  • More than 3 courses in one term is not recommended.
  • Fall and Spring terms should be consecutive. An empty Fall or Spring in the middle of your plan is a semester off, which needs the program director's approval. Summer is optional, so an empty summer is fine.
  • A course marked Unconfirmed has no schedule information for that term yet. Check before you register.
  • HINF 5101 belongs in your first term and HINF 7701 in your last.

7. When you're done

When the tracker says “This plan meets the degree requirements”, click Print roadmap. To keep a digital copy, choose Save as PDF in the print dialog. The roadmap lists every term, the requirement each course meets, any notes, and lines for you and your advisor to sign.

Edit setup changes your program version, start term or completed courses and keeps the rest of your plan. Log out erases everything.
